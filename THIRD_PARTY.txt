Hearthloop uses third-party software:

FFmpeg (ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe) is NOT included with Hearthloop.
  The first time it's needed, Hearthloop offers to download the official Windows build
  from gyan.dev (https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/) into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Hearthloop\ffmpeg,
  or you can use an ffmpeg you already have. FFmpeg is a separate program that Hearthloop runs;
  that build is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3 (its licence text is saved
  next to it as FFMPEG_LICENSE.txt). Source: https://ffmpeg.org  and  https://github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg
