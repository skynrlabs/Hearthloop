Hearthloop Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Hearthloop is a Windows app by Skynr Labs that turns your music and a background image into an animated ambient video. This policy covers the Hearthloop app, including the version sold on the Microsoft Store and on itch.io and the free demo.

The short version

Hearthloop doesn't collect, store or send any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your music, images, projects and videos stay on your computer.

What stays on your computer

  • Your songs, images, projects (.hloop files) and finished videos are saved only where you choose to save them.
  • Settings, the song library and Hearthloop's built-in ambience sounds are kept in your Windows user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Hearthloop).
  • None of this is uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes it. With the itch.io installer version, you can delete that folder after uninstalling.

The one time Hearthloop goes online

Hearthloop uses ffmpeg, a free video tool, to make videos. The first time it's needed, Hearthloop asks to download the official Windows build of ffmpeg from gyan.dev, and only does so if you agree. Like any download, this connects your computer to gyan.dev's server, which sees your IP address. Hearthloop sends nothing else about you or your work. After that, Hearthloop works offline.

Links that open your browser

The Help menu has links to the Skynr Labs Discord and to Hearthloop's GitHub page for bug reports. Clicking one opens your web browser. Hearthloop itself sends nothing. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Purchases

If you buy Hearthloop, the purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store or itch.io under their own privacy policies. Skynr Labs doesn't receive your payment details.

Children

Hearthloop doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Ask in the Skynr Labs Discord or open an issue on GitHub.
